02 / ABOUT
JP Han explores the intersection of Asian calligraphy, abstract symbolism, and the human form. HanScript is a personal visual language shaped by Hangul, Hanja, ancient manuscripts, and contemporary expression. Each composition is created to flow naturally with the body, embracing asymmetry, balance, and beauty in imperfection.
